Problem Statement

MotorIQ – Smart Vehicle Marketplace Platform

1. Background

The used vehicle market in Sri Lanka is fragmented across scattered classified advertisements, social media groups, and informal dealer networks. Buyers face challenges in verifying vehicle authenticity, comparing fair prices, and communicating efficiently with sellers. Sellers, in turn, lack a professional, trustworthy platform to showcase their vehicles effectively. There is a significant gap for a modern, intelligent, and secure vehicle marketplace that leverages technology to enhance the buying and selling experience.

2. Problem Definition

Existing vehicle selling platforms in Sri Lanka suffer from several key issues:

  • Lack of Trust: No standardized trust or verification scoring system for sellers and listings.
  • Inefficient Communication: Buyers and sellers must rely on external messaging apps, leading to scattered conversations without context.
  • No Price Intelligence: Buyers have no way to determine whether a listed price is fair for a given vehicle's specifications, age, and mileage.
  • Poor Search & Discovery: Limited filtering and no smart recommendation capabilities make vehicle discovery cumbersome.
  • No Centralized Management: Lack of admin moderation tools leads to spam, fraudulent listings, and a poor user experience.
  • No Mobile-First Experience: Most existing platforms are web-only, offering a subpar mobile experience in a mobile-first country.

3. Proposed Solution

MotorIQ is a full-stack, intelligent vehicle marketplace platform designed to solve these problems through:

3.1 Core Features

  • User Authentication & Security: Email OTP verification, secure login with JWT and password management.
  • Vehicle Listings Management: Create, edit, and delete vehicle listings with multi-image upload, watermarking, and detailed specifications.
  • AI-Powered Price Estimation: Intelligent price prediction using Google Generative AI based on vehicle brand, model, year, mileage, and condition.
  • Trust Score System: Automated trust score calculation based on listing completeness, seller history, and verification status.
  • Real-Time Chat: Socket.IO-powered messaging between buyers and sellers with image attachments, read receipts, reply threads, and typing indicators.
  • Smart Search & Recommendations: Multi-criteria filtered search with AI-driven vehicle recommendations based on similarity matching.
  • Reviews & Ratings: Buyers can leave reviews and ratings for vehicles they've interacted with.
  • Favorites System: Users can save and manage a list of favorite vehicles for quick access.
  • Premium Boosting: Sellers can boost their listings to premium status via deposit slip upload and admin approval.
  • Admin Dashboard: Full administrative control with user management (ban/unban, admin privileges), vehicle moderation, boost request approval, and broadcast notifications.
  • Real-Time Notifications: Socket-based push notifications for messages, system alerts, price updates, and admin broadcasts.
  • Loan Calculator: Built-in financial calculator for estimating monthly payments.

3.2 Technology Stack

  • Frontend (Web): React.js (Vite), TailwindCSS
  • Frontend (Mobile): React Native (Expo)
  • Backend: Node.js, Express.js
  • Database: MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
  • Real-Time: Socket.IO
  • File Storage: Multer (local uploads with watermarking)
  • Authentication: JWT, bcrypt, Nodemailer (OTP), Google OAuth
  • Deployment: Render (Backend), Vercel/Netlify (Frontend)

4. Objectives

  • Build a secure, scalable full-stack vehicle marketplace.
  • Provide AI-driven pricing intelligence and vehicle recommendations.
  • Enable real-time buyer-seller communication with rich messaging features.
  • Implement a trust-based ecosystem to reduce fraud and improve user confidence.
  • Deliver a responsive web application and a native mobile experience.
  • Provide comprehensive admin tools for platform moderation and management.

5. Target Users

  • Buyers: Individuals looking to purchase new or used vehicles with transparency and intelligence.
  • Sellers: Vehicle owners or dealers who want a professional platform to list and promote their vehicles.
  • Administrators: Platform managers who moderate listings, manage users, and oversee platform health.
